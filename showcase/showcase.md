# Carom

A clean court. Two paddles. No straight answers. Carom is a quick-fire paddle
duel where a moving strike puts a curve on your shot. Pick Solo to face the
computer, or take opposite sides of one keyboard in Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your position. Catch it in the
center for a controlled return, or reach with the paddle's edge to send it
climbing. Every paddle hit adds speed. Soon, a comfortable exchange becomes a
race to read the next angle.

Moving at the instant of contact gives the ball spin. Watch its pale trail bend
across the dark court, then use that curve to pull a return away from your
opponent. The two gold obstacles sway and turn together: an opening one moment,
a bank-shot opportunity the next.

First to eleven takes the match, but you need a two-point lead. At ten-all,
there's no clock to rescue you—just another serve, another moving target, and
one more chance to make the angle yours.
